### Business need and scope

Reduce the time drivers spend finding parking and provide operators with tools to improve occupancy, pricing and utilization. Deliver a coordinated web/mobile platform supporting secure accounts, vehicle profiles, current garage and space availability, maps, search and filtering, reservations and extensions, digital payment and refunds, receipts, directions, notifications and operator reporting. Exclude construction or physical operation of facilities, sensor manufacturing or installation, cash payments, citations, autonomous parking and ownership of external services.

### Deliverables and acceptance

1. Web and mobile applications, secured backend APIs and database.
2. Parking data, mapping, payment and notification integrations.
3. Operator dashboard, inventory and pricing controls, occupancy and revenue reporting.
4. SRS, competitive analysis, acquisition brief, WBS/Gantt, backlog, risk, communication, resource/cost and governance plans.
5. Test and acceptance evidence, deployment and support documentation, closure report and maintenance proposal.

Acceptance requires evidence against the SRS, stakeholder review and sponsor sign-off; PM authorizes release only after required approvals. Funding and development resources are available as needed, but estimates are tracked; the timeline remains fixed and external services, security, privacy and applicable city requirements constrain delivery.

### Engagement strategy

- Manage closely: milestone demos, approval reviews, issue escalation, consultation on operational requirements.
- Keep satisfied: confirm service contracts, integration requirements, material incidents and changes.
- Keep informed: regular progress communication, usability feedback and defect reviews appropriate to role.
- Monitor: reassess classifications when influence or interest changes.
